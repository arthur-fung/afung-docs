Android crash trend

Scribe Android (com.athelas.scribe), crash events only, six Mon–Sun weeks, Aug 31 – Oct 11 2026. Source: Firebase Crashlytics (project athelas-scribe-mobile), read 2026-10-15.

  • Foreground service (ForegroundServiceDidNotStopInTimeException, muted since 3/31) is a flat floor: 17–27 events and 16–24 users every week, 122 events and 70 users over six weeks.
  • Intune / MAM crashes are the spikes: zero in Sep 7–13, then 102 events the next week (76% of that week's crashes), 14, 54, and 10 in the latest week. Something changed around Sep 14; the cause is not yet known.
  • The two bad crash-free-user weeks (96.34% and 97.61%) are the two MAM spike weeks.
