Paused recording sent to MDS

Support ticket 570799 · iOS app 3.38.0 · recording on 2026-09-17 · investigated 2026-10-27

Short answer: the recording was ended, not paused.

The provider recorded for 6 min 25 s without pausing, then the app logged recording ended. On this account an ended recording always goes to MDS. Pause itself worked normally all day: no paused recording was sent on its own.

1. What they're asking

The provider says: "When I pause a recording to finish it later, it gets sent to the scribe/MDS team before I've finished. Auto-send is off. Why, and how do we stop it?"

Support's theory was that the app sent the recording without the provider ending it, because they couldn't find a PARALLEL_RECORDING_ENDED event for it.

2. What I checked

  1. The scribe row for the recording (…5327fd) on the Postgres replica: when it was created, its audio length, status and app version.
  2. Every app event for this recording in scribe_events, from 9/15 to 9/17.
  3. The same provider's other recordings that day, to compare against what normal looks like.

3. What happened to this recording, step by step

Time (UTC)What happened
17:23:31The provider started recording.
17:23 → 17:29Recorded for 6 min 25 s. Never paused. Audio levels show normal speech (average −33 dBFS).
17:29:56The app logged recording ended (385 s recorded).
17:29:58The audio uploaded, the scribe was created and the local copy was deleted.
17:30:06The note was sent to MDS.

The recording ended event is there, so support's theory doesn't hold. Nothing suggests an automatic upload: no app kill, no background event, no cache recovery.

4. Is that normal for this provider?

Yes. That day the provider ended 17 recordings, and every one went through the same steps:

recording ended → uploaded → sent to MDS (6–14 s later)

The provider also paused 13 times that day, and every pause worked:

RecordingWhat the app loggedSent to MDS?
Ticket recordingstart → end at 6:25 (no pause)Yes, because it was ended
Example Astart → pause → resumed 2.4 h later → endOnly after it was ended
Example Bstart → pause → resume → pause → resume → endOnly after it was ended
Example Cstart → pause → nothing elseNo, it stayed paused

5. Conclusion

  • Pause works. No paused recording was sent on its own.
  • This recording wasn't paused. It was ended after 6.5 minutes, most likely because End (or Send) was tapped instead of Pause.
  • On this account, ending a recording always sends it to MDS. The auto-send setting doesn't change that.
  • Can't rule out: the app logs that a recording ended, not what ended it. A user tap and an end triggered by the app look the same in today's data.

6. Next steps

  1. Support: ask the provider which button they pressed and on which screen. A confusing or misplaced button would explain repeat reports.
  2. Engineering: add the trigger to the recording ended event (user tap vs. app) so cases like this can be confirmed. The same gap exists for pause events on web.
