What happens

Why a recording "pauses on its own" a few minutes in, and why the provider has to reopen the app.

1. RecordingMemory used by the app's audio path keeps growing (AFEP-2644: ~306 MB → 2.5 GB in ~5 min).
→
2. WarningiOS sends a low-memory warning. The app logs it (IOS_LOW_MEMORY_WARNING) but doesn't act on it.
→
3. Kill~2 min later iOS ends the app. No crash report is possible.
→
4. ReopenProvider reopens the app. It restores the recording as paused and logs APP_KILLED_DETECTED; Sentry files a WatchdogTermination.
→
5. NoteSome audio is lost; the note covers only the first few minutes.
