Recording funnel and where each metric sits

Every recording an app attempts ends in exactly one place. Each Q4 metric watches one exit or one defect. Stage widths are illustrative, not to scale.

Cross-cutting: app kills and crashes act on every stage

They are not an exit of their own. They push a recording into one of the exits above.

  • Kill, then recovery works: scribe lands with full audio, nothing counted.
  • Kill, user resumes, earlier audio missing: scribe lands (stage 4) but fails stage 5, so it shows up as a gap. This is the customer complaint.
  • Kill with an error event: counted as failed or excluded from Lost (APP_KILLED_DETECTED is one of the exclusions).
  • Kill, never resumed, cache left paused: Stuck in Cache. Kill with no trace at all: Lost.
  • Android crashes feed kills: foreground-service crash (~16 users/week) and the Intune/MAM group. Metric: Android crash-free users 98.73% → 99.4%.
